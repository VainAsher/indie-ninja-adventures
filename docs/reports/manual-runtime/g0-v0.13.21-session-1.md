## P0 Blockers (must fix before next G0 run)

### P0-G0-01 — Lantern Heights has no visual identity
Hub has no hub name display, no time-of-day representation, no theming that reads as the authored "Lantern Heights at dawn" setting. Tester had no sense of location or time.

**System:** hub rendering, HUD/UI, environmental art direction

### P0-G0-02 — Instructor Tai onboarding is toast-only, no character context
Opening sequence fires as rapid green-caps toast messages ("ACT 1 ONBOARDING: PRESS F1 FOR CONTROLS", "PRESS O TO OPEN MISSION BOARD" etc.). No dialogue UI, no character name card, no scene-setting. Tester did not know who Tai is or that instructions were coming from a named character.

**System:** CutsceneManager / DialogueManager wiring for Tai intro; CutsceneManager exists (Phase 2 complete) but Tai's intro is not wired as a cutscene

### P0-G0-03 — No automatic mission flow from spawn
G0 route assumes natural progression: spawn → Tai intro → social grounding objective appears. Actual: player must manually press O to open mission board and select mission. Mission world spawned is separate from the Lantern Heights hub.

**System:** mission flow wiring, tutorial trigger sequencing

### P0-G0-04 — Exit portal after social grounding goes to wrong world
After completing social grounding mission, going through the exit portal routes to another procedural world, not back to Lantern Heights hub. Expected: return to Lantern Heights central hub.

**System:** mission-return portal logic, `handleSoloPortalTravel`, `transition_type=mission_return`

### P0-G0-05 — Samson sparring mission soft-locks (most severe)
Mission has no defined opponent, no clear objective, no visible win condition, and critically no exit portal or return path to hub. Tester was stranded in the mission world.

**System:** sparring mission objective wiring, mission world layout (needs exit), NPC combat or sparring loop undefined

### P0-G0-06 — Linzi mission trigger broken
Linzi NPC dialogue does not trigger mission acceptance. Mission board shows Linzi waypoint missions but they cannot be completed. Mission flow from NPC interaction to acceptance is not wired correctly.

**System:** Linzi mission wiring, NPC → mission trigger path in GameScreen
