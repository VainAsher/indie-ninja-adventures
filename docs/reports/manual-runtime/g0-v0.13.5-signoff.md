## Metrics To Capture

Use the same scale for all five records.

- Time to first objective clarity: `<mm:ss>` from entering Campaign to understanding what to do next.
- First confusion point: exact route step and what the tester expected.
- Yin/Yang readability: `1-5` plus one sentence. `1` = not noticed, `5` = felt like companions.
- Lantern Heights home readability: `1-5` plus one sentence. `1` = generic level, `5` = clear home/community.
- Linzi praise/threat legibility: `1-5` plus one sentence. `1` = no read, `5` = useful/flattering but not overt villain.
- Hub-change noticed: `yes/no`, plus what was noticed.
- Save/reload result: `pass/fail`, with failure point if any.
- Overall route result: `pass/fail`.
