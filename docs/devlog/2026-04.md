## 2026-04-24

- **Version:** v0.12.06
- **System:** authoritative server lifecycle + replay determinism
- **What changed:** multiplayer join/rejoin ordering was hardened so duplicate handshakes do not churn slots, and replay input ordering now follows deterministic slot order each tick.
- **Why it matters to players:** reconnect/overlap edge cases are more stable in hosted sessions, and replay/debug behavior is more predictable when testing progression-critical mission flows.

- **Version:** v0.12.08
- **System:** Yin/Yang stance movement + duality prototype (P1-03A)
- **What changed:** Movement, traversal, and Echo abilities now express your stance. Yin play is measured and quiet — slower run, shorter dash, silent teleport, no noise on Echo spawn. Yang play is aggressive and loud — faster run, longer dash, ThunderStep stuns nearby enemies on arrival, Riot Echo blasts noise when it appears. Flow (balanced + recently active) unlocks HarmonicStep: a fast, quiet teleport that rewards players who mix stances without going idle. Enemy awareness now responds to noise, so stance choice has real stealth consequences.
- **Why it matters to players:** For the first time, picking a stance changes how you move and how the world reacts to you, not just which art you cast. A Yin run through a guard room feels different from a Yang charge — and Flow feels like genuine mastery rather than a bar to fill.
- **Suggested capture moment:** ThunderStep arrival stun on a cluster of enemies (Yang), followed by a silent Yin wall-climb escape while awareness timers tick down.
- **Confidence:** internal only (awaiting playtest validation)
