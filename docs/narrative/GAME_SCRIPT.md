# THE HOLLOWED NINJA — Full Game Script
**AAA Cinematic Game Script Format**

*See [`docs/GDD_NARRATIVE_FOUNDATION.md`](../GDD_NARRATIVE_FOUNDATION.md) for the origin story and allegorical map before reading this script.*

---

## TITLE SEQUENCE

FADE IN:

WIDE SHOT — A floating mountain range under dawn light. Lanterns drift like fireflies through the morning air.

**TITLE CARD: THE HOLLOWED NINJA**

Lantern chimes echo softly.

CUT TO:

---

## CUTSCENE 01 — OPENING: LANTERN HEIGHTS

CAMERA: Slow pan across the village. Ninja trainees practice forms. Merchants prepare stalls. Lanterns glow warm and golden.

PLAYER CHARACTER: AEN, young ninja, stands at a cliff balcony overlooking the world.

> **AEN (VO):** "In the world born of lanternlight, every soul carries a spark. Mine was twofold… Yin and Yang… my guiding spirits."

Two small glowing orbs float around him — one soft silver, one warm gold.

NPC: INSTRUCTOR TAI enters.

> **INSTRUCTOR TAI:** "Today you begin your true path, Aen. A ninja of the Lantern Clan must forge his own legacy."

> **AEN:** "I am ready."

> **TAI (smiling):** "Then greet the village. They are your allies. Your home."

**OBJECTIVE PROMPT:** Explore Lantern Heights. Meet the villagers.

---

## HUB SCENE 1 — LANTERN HEIGHTS (ACT 0 HUB)

**NPC INTERACTION — MERCHANT RILU**
> **RILU:** "Fresh steel! Fresh cloth! You'll need both if you're taking on missions."

**NPC INTERACTION — SMITH JENRO**
> **JENRO:** "Bring me what you find out there. I'll forge you the tools you deserve."

**NPC INTERACTION — SAMSON**
> **SAMSON:** "Aen! You're finally ranked. Knew you had the spark."
> "If you run missions with that Maiden, watch your back. Her smile is… sharp."
> "Yin and Yang like you more than the lantern cats do. That's talent."

**NPC INTERACTION — SOPHIA**
> **SOPHIA:** "I've charted most of the Heights. But some paths only reveal themselves to those who walk with honest intention."
> "Be careful with outsiders, Aen. Not all who glow are warm."

**NPC INTERACTION — MARCEL**
> **MARCEL:** "If you scrape that blade again, I'll replace it myself. Come here."
> "Anyone who praises you too quickly usually wants something."

**NPC INTERACTION — HAZEL**
> **HAZEL:** "Your spirits glow bright today. Are you excited… or nervous?"
> "If you need new lantern silk, I've been experimenting with star-thread."

**NPC INTERACTION — VEIL MAIDEN (FIRST APPEARANCE)**

CAMERA: Focus in. Soft shimmering aura around her.

> **VEIL MAIDEN:** "Aen… the prodigy of Lantern Heights. I have watched your training. You shine brighter than the rest."

> **AEN:** "You… know who I am?"

> **VEIL MAIDEN:** "Oh, I know your potential. Walk with me. I have a task only you can complete."

**MISSION UNLOCKED:** Veil Maiden's Request

---

## CUTSCENE 02 — VEIL MAIDEN'S FIRST MISSION

CAMERA: Forest path swallowed in mist.

> **VEIL MAIDEN:** "Deep in these woods, a corrupted beast stirs. The others are too cautious. But you… you are different."

> **AEN:** "I'll take care of it."

> **VEIL MAIDEN (smiling):** "I knew you would say that."

---

## IN-LEVEL DIALOGUE — EARLY MISSIONS

> **YANG (spirit):** "Aen, something feels strange with her…"

> **YIN (spirit):** "She clouds even our light."

> **AEN:** "She trusts me with important tasks. That must mean something."

---

## HUB SCENE 2 — NPC WITHDRAWAL

*Few NPCs remain. Shops close early.*

> **MERCHANT RILU:** "Sorry Aen… I just… need some distance. Things feel off around here."

> **AEN:** "Distance? From what?"

> **RILU (avoids eye contact):** "From her."

CAMERA: Veil Maiden stands behind Aen, smiling faintly.

---

## ACT 0 ISOLATION DIALOGUE

**SAMSON (fading presence)**
> "Hey… you look tired. You been doing only her missions again?"
> "Don't forget who you were before you met her, Aen."

**SOPHIA (rare appearance)**
> "The village feels off… like something is dimming the lanterns."
> "If I'm not around next time, just know — I wish you well."

**MARCEL (avoids direct conflict)**
> "Listen. Jenro closed his forge early. Everyone's uneasy. Be careful."
> "…Don't let her talk you into abandoning the rest of us."

**HAZEL (gentle warning)**
> "Aen… the way she watches you… it chills my lantern flame."
> "Don't disappear on us, okay?"

---

## CUTSCENE 03 — SUBTLE ISOLATION

CAMERA: Night. Aen walks through increasingly empty hub.

> **AEN (VO):** "Everyone was drifting away. And the more alone I became… the more she praised me."

---

## HUB SCENE 3 — JUST AEN AND THE VEIL MAIDEN

> **VEIL MAIDEN:** "Look around. They never believed in you. But I do."

> **AEN:** "I… don't understand why they left."

> **VEIL MAIDEN:** "Because you outshined them. You only need me now."

---

## FINAL NPC DEPARTURES

**SAMSON (serious, last time)**
> "You're my brother, Aen. But if you keep following her… I can't follow you."

**SOPHIA (resigned)**
> "She whispers poison. And you're breathing it in."

**MARCEL (angry)**
> "She's shutting us out. All of us. You need to see what's happening!"

**HAZEL (last lantern)**
> "Aen… I wish you knew how much you mattered. Even without her."

*After this, Aen finds the hub empty except for Linzi/The Veil Maiden.*

---

## CUTSCENE 04 — FIRST BOSS: THE SIREN OF MASKS

LOCATION: Summit Shrine of Lantern Heights.

CAMERA: Slow push-in as the Maiden's shimmer distorts.

> **VEIL MAIDEN (voice deepening):** "You gave me everything. Your trust. Your focus. Your isolation."

Her form twists into THE SIREN OF MASKS — a towering spirit-demon draped in screaming visages.

> **SIREN:** "Now… give me the rest."

> **AEN:** "W-What are you?!"

> **SIREN:** "I am what grows in the cracks of a lonely heart."

**BOSS FIGHT BEGINS**

*The Siren uses illusions of the lost NPCs to confuse the player. She drains the Lantern. Yin and Yang orbit frantically, trying to protect Aen.*

*The player loses. By design.*

---

## CUTSCENE 05 — SCRIPTED DEFEAT

AEN collapses. Yin and Yang struggle to defend him.

> **SIREN:** "Your spirits… your balance… your children of soul."

She rips Yin and Yang from him.

> **AEN:** "NO!"

> **SIREN:** "You are hollow now."

She casts him into darkness.

*Aen's sprite dims. Shoulders slump. Idle animation loses energy. He is now The Hollowed One.*

**FADE OUT.**

---

## CUTSCENE 06 — THE HOLLOWING (ACT 2 INTRO)

LOCATION: The Hollow Depths. Dim lantern fragments flicker weakly.

> **SHADE HERMIT:** "Awaken, Hollow One."

> **AEN (weak):** "My… spirits… they were taken…"

> **HERMIT:** "Then you must walk the dark until you find yourself again."

**OBJECTIVE:** Survive the Hollow Depths.

---

## IN-LEVEL DIALOGUE — HOLLOW DEPTHS

> **AEN:** "My body… it feels heavy."

> **HERMIT (echoing):** "Depression is weight without shape. You must push through it."

---

## SHADOW-ECHO DIALOGUE (Hollow Depths — Memory Fragments)

*These echoes do not condemn Aen. They mourn.*

**SHADOW-ECHO OF SAMSON**
> "You weren't alone… why did you walk away?"
> "I couldn't reach you."

**SHADOW-ECHO OF SOPHIA**
> "I wanted to help… but she turned your eyes from me."

**SHADOW-ECHO OF MARCEL**
> "You pushed so hard… until you broke."

**SHADOW-ECHO OF HAZEL**
> "You never stopped being worth saving."

---

## BOSS INTRO — THE WEIGHTBOUND OGRE

*Symbolises sleep deprivation, exhaustion, the endless grind of simply continuing to exist.*

> **SHADE HERMIT (before fight):** "This one feeds on weariness. The longer the fight, the stronger it grows. You must be decisive."

---

## CUTSCENE 07 — FIRST SPARKS

LOCATION: Hollow Hub. A faint lantern lights.

NPC: SMITH MONK appears.

> **SMITH MONK:** "You still fight. Good. Let me rebuild what was lost."

**UNLOCK:** Dash Restored.

---

## BOSS INTRO — THE SHATTER MOTH QUEEN

*Symbolises manipulation, gaslighting, the emotional hostility that follows you after.*

> **SHADE HERMIT:** "She does not fight you directly. She makes you doubt what you see. Trust your own eyes."

---

## CUTSCENE 08 — THE STONE JUDGE

LOCATION: Shifting stone maze.

> **STONE JUDGE (VO):** "You seek justice? You shall earn nothing."

> **AEN:** "I seek only my spirits back."

> **STONE JUDGE:** "Then you shall suffer."

**BOSS INTRO — STONE JUDGE**

*Symbolises the family court system. Rigid, immovable, indifferent.*

*He cannot be damaged from the front. He must be outmanoeuvred.*

---

## ILLUSORY WHISPERS — VEIL MAIDEN (Acts 2–4)

*Small illusions appear throughout the Hollow Depths and Skyroad. They shatter when hit.*

> "You were nothing without me."
> "They abandoned you long before I did."
> "Why keep climbing? They won't come back."
> "Stop fighting. Come fall again."

---

## CUTSCENE 09 — FRACTURED CONTACT

*High winds. Collapsing platforms.*

> **AEN (VO):** "I could feel their light… once. But every time I reached… the world broke beneath me."

**UNLOCK:** Double Jump Restored.

---

## CUTSCENE 10 — FINAL CUTOFF

LOCATION: Abyssal Gate.

AEN sees Yin and Yang being pulled into darkness.

> **AEN:** "Yin! Yang! Please—!"

The gate slams shut. AEN collapses.

> **HERMIT:** "When a path closes… another must open."

**UNLOCK:** Glide.

---

## CUTSCENE 11 — HEARTH OF BROTHERS (ACT 3 HUB TRANSITION)

Warm lights bloom. Figures gather.

> **BROTHER KAI:** "You are not alone, Hollow One."

> **BROTHER LEN:** "We walk together now."

> **AEN:** "Why help me?"

> **KAI:** "Because someone once helped us. Sit. Speak. Heal."

**UNLOCK:** AirDodge, GrappleHook.

## CUTSCENE 12 — EMBER MONASTERY TRAINING

LOCATION: Massive dojo atop a warm mountain sanctuary.

> **MENTOR ROGA:** "Strength is not the absence of pain. It is mastery over it."

**UNLOCK:** WallCling.

## HUB SCENE — SAMSON RETURNS

*He walks into the hub one day.*

> **SAMSON:** "You fell far, brother. But I'm here now. And I'm not leaving again."

> **AEN:** "Samson… you came back."

> **SAMSON:** "I never stopped looking for you."

*He becomes the combat mentor.*

---

## HUB SCENE — MARCEL RETURNS (Act 3)

> **MARCEL:** "Knew you weren't finished. Let me reinforce your armor. No more breaking."
> "If the world hit you this hard, hit it back twice."

---

## HUB SCENE — SOPHIA (Guest Appearance, Act 3)

> **SOPHIA:** "I heard your lantern shone again… so I came."
> "You've changed, Aen. The good kind of change."

*She provides maps for the Skyroad ascent.*

---

## HUB SCENE — HAZEL (Throughout Ember Monastery)

> "You grew… so much."
> "When you light that beacon, I'll be right behind you."

---

## CUTSCENE 13 — SKYROAD ASCENT

LOCATION: Towering vertical ascent into the sky.

> **HERMIT:** "Your spirits are beyond reach. But your path is not."

> **AEN:** "I will climb. For them. For myself."

*Yin and Yang appear in background scenes as shimmering stars — distant, watching.*

---

## CUTSCENE 14 — FINAL BOSS: THE HOLLOW REFLECTION

LOCATION: Summit of the world. A dark mirror of Aen forms — dripping shadow, hollow eyes.

> **HOLLOW REFLECTION:** "I am what you would have become. Broken. Bitter. Lost."

> **AEN:** "No. You are what I leave behind."

**FINAL FIGHT BEGINS**

*To defeat the Reflection, every ability earned from the community must be used. Not raw strength — the accumulated weight of help received and given. He cannot be beaten alone.*

---

## CUTSCENE 15 — VICTORY

*The Reflection fades into dust.*

> **AEN:** "I release you."

*He walks to the Beacon.*

---

## FINAL NPC REACTIONS (Before Ascent)

> **SAMSON:** "Go. Light the sky. Give Yin and Yang something to find."

> **SOPHIA:** "This ascent… it's your story written in the clouds."

> **MARCEL:** "Show the world the man you became."

> **HAZEL (final whisper):** "I'm proud of you, Aen."

---

## ENDING CINEMATIC — THE BEACON OF RETURN

*Aen lights the ancient Beacon. A massive golden flare illuminates the world.*

*Two stars appear in the sky — Yin and Yang.*

*They do not descend. They shine brighter.*

> **AEN (softly):** "When you seek me… I will be here."

*The narrator (or his own inner voice, quietly):*

> "Yin and Yang do not return to those who beg for them. They return to those who become a home for them. The Hollowed Ninja is no more. You are whole. And one day… when the time is right… the spirits will find you in the light."

**FADE TO SUNRISE.**

**END.**

## SIDE QUEST CHAINS

### Samson — "The Spear at Your Back"

**Quest 1: "Unfinished Sparring Match"**
When: Early Lantern Heights (Act 0)
Hook: Samson challenges Aen to a friendly duel.
Objective: Enter a small dojo room and defeat Samson's training ghost in a timed combat challenge.
Reward: Small permanent +HP or +poise buff.
Story beat: Samson is the first to show he believes in Aen's potential without flattery.

**Quest 2: "Echoes in the Dark"**
When: Hollow Depths (Act 2, after first boss)
Hook: Aen hears Samson's echo more clearly in a certain level.
Objective: Follow echo waypoints through optional rooms. Defeat a Guilt Wraith using Samson's voice to taunt Aen.
Wraith dialogue: "You abandoned me." / "You chose her over us."
Reward: Samson Charm — bonus when at low health.
Story beat: Aen acknowledges the drift. Samson's memory becomes strength, not shame.

**Quest 3: "The Brother Returns"**
When: Ember Monastery (Act 3)
Hook: Full reunion cutscene.
Objective: Optional AI co-op trial — Samson fights beside you.
Reward: Passive bonus when fighting groups ("Samson at your back").

---

### Sophia — "The Ink That Remembers"

**Quest 1: "Lantern Cartography"**
When: Act 0–1
Hook: Sophia asks Aen to collect fragments of old maps in early missions.
Objective: Find 3–5 Map Shards in dungeons.
Reward: Minimap upgrade.

**Quest 2: "Slander and Silence"**
When: Late Act 0–Act 1
Hook: Sophia begins to disappear. Whispers spread — the Maiden has spoken ill of her.
Objective: Read 2–3 overheard rumours from minor NPCs.
Reward: A Letter from Sophia explaining why she chose to leave to protect herself.
Story beat: Mirrors the ex trying to slander a close friend. Sophia chose survival, not abandonment.

**Quest 3: "Map to the Skyroad"**
When: Late Ember Monastery / start of Skyroad (Act 3–4)
Hook: Sophia returns briefly as a traveller.
Objective: Fetch "Star Ink" from a rare location.
Reward: Skyroad Map — improves route clarity, possibly unlocks a secret shortcut.

---

### Marcel — "The Iron Voice"

**Quest 1: "Guard the Forge"**
When: Lantern Heights (Act 0–1)
Hook: Marcel asks Aen to guard the forge while he and Jenro gather materials.
Objective: Defend the forge from enemy waves (timed).
Reward: Early weapon enhancement (+1 damage tier).

**Quest 2: "Broken Steel, Unbroken Will"**
When: Hollow Depths (Act 2)
Hook: Aen finds a broken hammer with Marcel's sigil.
Objective: Retrieve lost fragments from a dangerous side path.
Reward: Marcel's Hammer Charm — improved stagger against heavy enemies.

**Quest 3: "Reforging the Self"**
When: Ember Monastery (Act 3)
Hook: Marcel appears as a smith helping the Monastery.
Objective: Collect rare ore from a tough side-level.
Reward: Main weapon upgraded to "Ember Forged" final form.

---

### Hazel — "The Lantern Weaver"

**Quest 1: "A Gentle Glow"**
When: Early game
Hook: Hazel offers to weave protective lantern cloth.
Objective: Gather soft materials from low-threat creatures/plants.
Reward: Slightly increased healing from items/shrines.

**Quest 2: "Shreds in the Dark"**
When: Hollow Depths (mid-game)
Hook: A torn piece of Hazel's cloth appears in a dark level.
Objective: Retrieve it from a dangerous zone.
Reward: New Lantern Skin (cosmetic glow) + mild light radius buff.

**Quest 3: "Lantern for Tomorrow"**
When: Just before final ascent (Act 3–4)
Hook: Hazel is in the Ember Monastery hub, finishing a special lantern.
Objective: Bring her one last rare material from an optional high-skill challenge.
Reward: Beacon Lantern — used in the final cutscene, plus all-stats buff for final act.
